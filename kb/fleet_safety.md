# Fleet Safety Policy

This policy applies to every driver and every vehicle of the fleet.

## Speed limits

The fleet speed limit is 130 km/h on motorways without a posted limit, even
where German law allows faster driving. Vans above 3.5 tonnes and all trucks,
such as the Mercedes-Benz Actros and MAN TGX, are legally limited to 80 km/h on
motorways and must not exceed it. Posted limits always take priority. Trucks
have speed limiters set to 90 km/h; a truck reporting much higher speeds either
has a manipulated limiter or a measurement error, and both must be investigated.

## Loads

Loads must be secured before departure. Overloaded vehicles brake worse and
speed up on downhill sections; drivers of loaded trucks should use the engine
brake or retarder on long descents, such as the Kassel hills on the A7.

## Accidents

After an accident the driver secures the scene, calls emergency services if
anyone is hurt and informs dispatch. The fleet coordinator retrieves the
telemetry of the 30 minutes before the accident and keeps it for the insurer.
Telemetry is retained for 90 days, so it must be exported in time.

## Fatigue

Drivers must take the breaks required by law and must not drive when tired.
Dispatch must not plan routes that cannot be driven within the legal driving
hours. A long idle period during a break is acceptable; a long trip without any
break is not.

## Tampering

Disabling, covering or jamming the tracker, or manipulating the speed limiter or
tachograph, is forbidden and leads to disciplinary action. GPS jammers are also
illegal under German telecommunications law.
