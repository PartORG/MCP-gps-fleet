# Incident #1650: Suspected GPS jammer

- Vehicle: FM-0301 (Mercedes-Benz Actros)
- Date: 2026-06-11
- Alert type: gps_jump
- Severity: high
- Status: closed

## Observed
Position froze for 40 minutes near Leipzig while the odometer kept counting, then
jumped 28 km to a new location. Similar gaps occurred on three trips, always with
the same driver and always around unscheduled stops.

## Investigation
Other vehicles on the same roads had no problems. The gaps started and ended with
the driver's shifts. A small device plugged into the cigarette lighter socket was
found in the cab.

## Root cause
Deliberate GPS jamming by the driver with an illegal privacy device.

## Resolution
Device removed and handed to the fleet manager. Disciplinary procedure started.
