# Incident #1977: Fleet-wide position errors during a solar storm

- Vehicle: several (FM-0740, FM-0109, FM-0520 and others)
- Date: 2026-05-12
- Alert type: gps_jump
- Severity: low
- Status: closed

## Observed
Within four hours, 19 gps_jump alerts on 14 different vehicles across all of
Germany. Jumps were 10-15 km and returned quickly.

## Investigation
No common location, driver or vehicle model. The space weather service had
announced a strong geomagnetic storm for that day.

## Root cause
Ionospheric disturbance caused by a solar storm, degrading accuracy for all
receivers.

## Resolution
No action required. Alerts from that period were excluded from driver scoring.
