# Incident #1433: Fuel theft at a truck stop near Kassel

- Vehicle: FM-0977 (Iveco Daily)
- Date: 2026-04-16
- Alert type: fuel_drop
- Severity: high
- Status: closed

## Observed
The fuel level fell from 71 % to 44 % between 02:10 and 02:30 while the vehicle
was parked overnight at a truck stop on the A7 near Kassel, ignition off. The
level stayed low afterwards.

## Investigation
No refuel or fuel card transaction. The driver found scratches on the tank cap
in the morning. The drop happened while the vehicle was not moving.

## Root cause
Fuel theft (siphoning) at an unfenced parking area.

## Resolution
Police report filed and insurer informed. Locking fuel cap installed. Drivers on
this route now park at the fenced depot in Kassel.
