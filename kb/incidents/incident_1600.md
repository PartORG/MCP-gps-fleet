# Incident #1600: Refrigerated van idling during deliveries

- Vehicle: FM-0183 (Mercedes-Benz Sprinter)
- Date: 2026-06-30
- Alert type: long_idle
- Severity: low
- Status: closed

## Observed
Daily long_idle alerts of 45-75 minutes at supermarket deliveries in Munich.

## Investigation
FM-0183 carries a refrigeration unit driven by the engine. The engine has to run
during deliveries to keep the cargo cold.

## Root cause
Expected behaviour of a refrigerated vehicle.

## Resolution
No action required. Long idle alerts from refrigerated vans are now reviewed only
in the weekly report.
