# Incident #1208: Repeated GPS jumps caused by a loose antenna connector

- Vehicle: FM-0550 (Volkswagen Crafter)
- Date: 2026-03-19
- Alert type: gps_jump
- Severity: medium
- Status: closed

## Observed
Seven gps_jump alerts in five days at different places (Dortmund, Essen, on the
A1 and at the depot). Jumps of 10-25 km, each returning after 30-60 seconds.

## Investigation
The pattern was not tied to any location, so signal reflections and tunnels were
ruled out. The workshop found the GPS antenna connector behind the dashboard
loose and slightly corroded.

## Root cause
Hardware fault: loose and corroded antenna connector causing intermittent signal
loss.

## Resolution
Connector and antenna cable replaced. No further jumps in the following month.
