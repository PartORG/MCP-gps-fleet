# Incident #1820: Fuel reading drop on a sloped parking place

- Vehicle: FM-0270 (Ford Transit)
- Date: 2026-08-24
- Alert type: fuel_drop
- Severity: high
- Status: closed

## Observed
The fuel level dropped 17 % after the van parked at a customer site in Stuttgart
and returned to normal within minutes after it left.

## Investigation
The customer's yard is on a steep slope. The level recovered without refuel as
soon as the vehicle was on level ground.

## Root cause
Sensor reading affected by the vehicle's inclination.

## Resolution
No action required. Location noted as a known source of false fuel alerts.
