# Incident #1560: Repeated speeding on the A7

- Vehicle: FM-0012 (MAN TGE)
- Date: 2026-05-28
- Alert type: speeding
- Severity: high
- Status: closed

## Observed
Speeds between 150 and 168 km/h on the A7 between Hanover and Kassel on four
trips within two weeks.

## Investigation
Speeds were sustained over many samples and the positions were consistent, so a
measurement error was ruled out. Same driver on all trips. No urgent delivery had
been requested by dispatch.

## Root cause
Real speeding by the driver to gain time on long routes.

## Resolution
Documented coaching session (second event in 90 days); driver booked for a
safety training. Route planning for that line was given more time.
