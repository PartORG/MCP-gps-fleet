# Incident #1888: Speeding during an urgent delivery

- Vehicle: FM-0935 (Mercedes-Benz Vito)
- Date: 2026-08-29
- Alert type: speeding
- Severity: medium
- Status: closed

## Observed
Speeds of 140-148 km/h on the A9 between Leipzig and Nuremberg.

## Investigation
Dispatch had asked the driver to deliver hospital supplies as fast as possible.
The driver was not aware that the fleet limit also applies to urgent deliveries.

## Root cause
Unclear instruction from dispatch.

## Resolution
Informal conversation with the driver. Dispatch reminded that urgent deliveries
must be planned within the 130 km/h fleet limit.
