# Incident #1777: Diesel leak from a cracked fuel line

- Vehicle: FM-0862 (Iveco Daily)
- Date: 2026-08-11
- Alert type: fuel_drop
- Severity: high
- Status: closed

## Observed
Consumption was about twice the normal value over a full day of driving; the
platform raised a fuel_drop alert after the cumulative loss passed 15 %. The
driver reported a smell of diesel.

## Investigation
The loss was spread over many samples, also while driving, unlike a theft. The
vehicle was taken out of service and towed to the workshop.

## Root cause
Cracked fuel line at the filter housing.

## Resolution
Fuel line replaced, vehicle status set to maintenance during the repair and back
to active afterwards.
