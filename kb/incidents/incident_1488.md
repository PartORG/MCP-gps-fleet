# Incident #1488: Waiting at the loading dock in the port of Hamburg

- Vehicle: FM-0798 (Mercedes-Benz Sprinter)
- Date: 2026-04-28
- Alert type: long_idle
- Severity: low
- Status: closed

## Observed
Engine idling for 115 minutes at a container terminal in the port of Hamburg.

## Investigation
The customer's loading dock was congested and vehicles had to move forward in a
queue every few minutes, so the driver kept the engine running.

## Root cause
Waiting time at the customer, not a driver fault.

## Resolution
Waiting times raised with the customer; deliveries moved to an earlier time slot.
