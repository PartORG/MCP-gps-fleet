# Incident #1390: Idling to heat the cab during breaks

- Vehicle: FM-0798 (Mercedes-Benz Sprinter)
- Date: 2026-01-22
- Alert type: long_idle
- Severity: low
- Status: closed

## Observed
Three long_idle alerts in one week, each between 60 and 90 minutes, always during
the driver's lunch break at temperatures below zero.

## Investigation
The vehicle has a parking heater, but the driver did not know how to use it.

## Root cause
Idling to keep the cab warm during breaks.

## Resolution
Driver shown how to use the parking heater, which uses about a tenth of the fuel.
