# Incident #1519: Faulty fuel level sensor

- Vehicle: FM-0115 (Volkswagen Crafter)
- Date: 2026-05-05
- Alert type: fuel_drop
- Severity: high
- Status: closed

## Observed
The fuel level dropped by 22 % between two samples while driving and was back at
the previous level one hour later without any refuel. Similar drops occurred on
three days.

## Investigation
Drops in both directions, no fuel card mismatch, consumption over the week was
normal. The workshop found the float of the tank sensor sticking.

## Root cause
Faulty fuel level sensor (sticking float).

## Resolution
Sensor replaced. No theft; alerts dismissed.
