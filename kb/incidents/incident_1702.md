# Incident #1702: Truck exceeding its limiter on a downhill section

- Vehicle: FM-0655 (MAN TGX)
- Date: 2026-07-21
- Alert type: speeding
- Severity: high
- Status: closed

## Observed
The fully loaded truck reported 134 km/h on the A7 descent south of Kassel,
although trucks are legally limited to 80 km/h and the limiter is set to 90 km/h.

## Investigation
The limiter only cuts engine power; it does not brake. On the long descent the
heavy load accelerated the truck. The driver had not used the retarder.

## Root cause
Downhill acceleration of a loaded truck without using the engine brake, combined
with a limiter set without the downhill speed control function.

## Resolution
Downhill speed control activated in the workshop. Driver coached on using the
retarder.
